# Master Prompt — Build a World-Class Commercial Website for **Akechi ERP**



## Objective



Design and develop a **world-class, enterprise-grade commercial website** for **Akechi ERP**, a next-generation AI-powered Enterprise Resource Planning platform. The website should position Akechi ERP as a premium global SaaS product competing with enterprise solutions while maintaining a modern, trustworthy, and conversion-focused experience.



The website should be suitable for:



* Enterprise customers

* SMBs

* Manufacturing companies

* Retail businesses

* Healthcare

* Construction

* Logistics

* Education

* Government

* Finance

* Professional Services



The website must look like it was designed by a team with **25+ years of experience in enterprise SaaS product design**, combining the quality of Fortune 500 companies with the usability of modern SaaS products.

# Quality Standards



Before considering the project complete:



* Audit every page for UI/UX consistency.

* Validate responsiveness across all supported breakpoints.

* Ensure visual consistency with the design system.

* Eliminate layout shifts and performance bottlenecks.

* Verify accessibility compliance.

* Optimize SEO across every page.

* Confirm all forms, navigation, CTAs, animations, and interactive elements function correctly.

* Conduct a final enterprise readiness review covering design, engineering, content, security, and performance.



## Final Deliverable



Produce a **world-class commercial website for Akechi ERP** that looks and feels like a premium global SaaS platform, inspires confidence among enterprise buyers, and is fully prepared for production deployment. The website should be visually exceptional, technically robust, SEO-optimized, accessible, scalable, and capable of serving as the company's primary digital presence for worldwide customers.
