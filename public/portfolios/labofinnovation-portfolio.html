Overview

Project Overview

Lab of Innovation is not one business — it is four. It teaches robotics to children from age six, partners with schools and colleges on industry-aligned degree programs, upskills corporate teams in Industry 4.0 technologies, and incubates robotics startups in a world-class lab. Each audience arrives with completely different questions, vocabulary, and buying journeys — and the platform had to serve them all without confusing anyone.

Akechi Webcraft designed and built the entire platform as a fast, animation-rich web application: dedicated journeys for students, institutions, corporates, and innovators; a built-in e-commerce store for robotics kits and components with cart and secure checkout; a consultation booking system with video, phone, and in-person options; success stories, certifications, galleries, and a 24/7 support hub. The result is a single digital home that presents a young company (est. 2025) with the polish and depth of an established enterprise.

Key Features

Our goal was one platform where four very different audiences — parents, educators, corporate leaders, and innovators — each find a tailored journey from first visit to enrollment, purchase, or partnership.

Project Challenges and Their Solutions

Building one platform for four audiences — from six-year-olds' parents to corporate decision-makers — brought real design friction. Here is how we solved it.

1. One Platform, Four Very Different Audiences

Challenges

A parent researching classes for their eight-year-old, a college dean evaluating a partnership, a corporate L&D head budgeting a training program, and a startup founder seeking incubation all land on the same homepage — with entirely different expectations, vocabulary, and decision criteria.

Solutions

We designed an audience-first information architecture: a universal hero that establishes credibility for everyone, followed by clearly signposted pathways into dedicated sections — each written in its audience's language, with its own CTAs, proof points, and forms. Shared elements like stats, team credentials, and certifications reinforce trust across every journey.

2. Selling Hands-On Learning Through a Screen

Challenges

Lab of Innovation's core value is physical: children building real robots, corporate teams touching industrial automation rigs, startups using ₹10 Crores+ of lab equipment. A static website risked flattening that tangible, hands-on energy into brochure copy.

Solutions

We made the site itself feel alive: motion-rich storytelling with animated counters, scroll-triggered reveals, and gradient energy throughout, paired with concrete proof — workshop photo galleries with lightbox viewing, student success stories like the AI crop-monitoring drone serving 500+ farms and the award-winning smart-city traffic system deployed in 3 cities.

3. Full E-Commerce Inside a Marketing Platform

Challenges

The robotics kit store needed real shopping functionality — product catalog, cart, checkout, order confirmation — without the cost and complexity of a separate e-commerce system, and without breaking the visual continuity of the platform.

Solutions

We built a native storefront within the application: product cards with detailed specs, cart state management, order notes, delivery instructions, and an encrypted checkout flow with clear security messaging — all sharing the platform's design system, so shopping feels like a natural extension of learning rather than a bolted-on shop.

4. Rich Animation Without Sacrificing Speed

Challenges

An education brand aimed at innovators must feel modern and dynamic — but animation libraries, galleries, forms, and store logic can bloat a single-page app into a slow first load, especially on the mobile devices most parents and students browse with.

Solutions

We engineered performance into the build: the React core and animation engine ship as separate cacheable vendor chunks loaded in parallel, fonts are preconnected, and animations run on GPU-friendly transforms. The platform keeps its kinetic, high-energy feel while staying fast on 4G connections.
