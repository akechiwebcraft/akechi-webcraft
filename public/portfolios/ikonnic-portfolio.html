Overview

Project Overview

Personalized gifting is an emotional purchase sold through a screen. A customer isn't buying an acrylic sheet — they're buying a wedding photo on their living-room wall, a child's name above a bedroom door, a memory on the fridge. Ikonnic's vision was to make that feeling shoppable: a store where anyone can turn a photo on their phone into premium décor in a few taps, and trust that what arrives matches what they imagined.

Akechi Webcraft designed and built the full Ikonnic experience: a photography-first storefront across 12 personalized product categories, a guided "Personalise Yours" customization flow on every product, integrated login, cart, and order tracking, and a content layer — blog, FAQ, and a photo-poses guide — that helps customers send better photos and buy with confidence. Behind it all sits a dual-hub fulfilment model, with a Creative Fulfilment Hub in Jaipur and a Print & Dispatch Studio in Bengaluru, routing every order to the nearest hub for a 3-business-day dispatch promise.

Key Features

Our goal was a store that sells feelings, not SKUs — where customization is effortless, trust is visible at every step, and made-to-order still means fast.

Project Challenges and Their Solutions

Selling made-to-order emotional products online brings problems a normal store never faces. Here is how we solved them.

1. Customers Aren't Designers — But Every Order Is a Design

Challenges

Every Ikonnic order starts with a customer's own photo. Low-resolution uploads, awkward crops, and poorly lit pictures produce disappointing prints — and in a personalized category, a disappointing print is a lost customer who can't even return the product.

Solutions

We designed a guided "Personalise Yours" flow with education built in: a step-by-step upload-preview-order journey on every product, backed by a photo-poses guide that shows customers exactly how to shoot pictures that print beautifully. The customer feels creative, not burdened — and the hub receives files worth printing.

2. Winning Trust in a Category With No Returns

Challenges

A personalized acrylic photo can't be restocked or resold, so the usual e-commerce safety net — easy returns — doesn't apply. Buyers paying ₹999–₹1,699 for something they can't send back need a different reason to click "order."

Solutions

We made trust the loudest element on the page: 10K+ happy customers, 50K+ prints delivered, and a 4.9★ rating surfaced up front; explicit promises of quality checks, secure packaging, and hassle-free resolutions; and human support one WhatsApp message away. The store replaces a returns policy with visible proof and reachable people.

3. Made-to-Order Speed Across a Country the Size of India

Challenges

Print-on-demand usually means slow: nothing exists until the order arrives, and a single production site means long transit to half the country. Gifting is deadline-driven — a birthday gift that arrives late has failed completely.

Solutions

The store is built around a dual-hub fulfilment model — a Creative Fulfilment Hub in Jaipur and a Print & Dispatch Studio in Bengaluru — with each order routed to the nearest hub. Combined with order tracking for post-purchase confidence, Ikonnic can promise dispatch within 3 business days on fully custom products, pan-India.

4. Twelve Categories Without a Cluttered Store

Challenges

Twelve customizable categories — each with sizes, finishes, and variants — can easily become a maze. Gifting customers often arrive with an occasion in mind, not a product, and an overwhelming catalog sends them back to the marketplace apps.

Solutions

We organized the store around a hero-product hierarchy: acrylic wall photos lead with the "Every wall can tell a story" hero, trending-gift rails surface proven sellers with prices visible, and gift bundles package decisions for occasion shoppers. Lifestyle photography does the explaining, so every category is understood at a glance instead of read.
