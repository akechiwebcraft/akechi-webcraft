Overview

Project Overview

Handcrafted leather goods sell on touch, texture, and story — three things a website cannot literally offer. RAQZ needed an online store that could communicate the weight of full-grain leather, the character of hand-tooling, and the honesty of artisan work, while still performing like a modern e-commerce business: fast pages, clear navigation, trustworthy checkout, and repeatable conversion.

Akechi Webcraft built raqz.in on Shopify with a custom-tailored storefront. The design pairs an earthy, editorial aesthetic with a rigorously structured catalog — trifold and bifold wallets, card-holders, shoulder bags, pouches, and duffels — supported by product storytelling, customer reviews, promotional sale mechanics, and pan-India free shipping. The result is a store that feels like the brand and sells like a platform.

Project Challenges and Their Solutions

Every premium brand build has its friction points. Here is how we solved RAQZ's.

1. Conveying Craftsmanship Through a Screen

Challenges

Full-grain and suede leather sell on touch and texture. Online, product photos alone risked making handcrafted goods look like mass-market alternatives, undermining the premium price point.

Solutions

We built the entire visual language around texture and story: full-bleed campaign photography, an earthy editorial palette, and narrative product copy describing origin, hand-tooling, and material. The site design itself signals craft, so the price point feels earned before the shopper reads a single specification.

2. Structuring a Diverse Catalog

Challenges

Wallets, card-holders, crossbody bags, pouches, and duffels span very different price bands (₹799 to ₹8,999) and buyer intents. A flat product list would bury high-margin hero products and confuse first-time visitors.

Solutions

We designed a collection-first taxonomy — seven curated collections surfaced directly in the main navigation — with a Bestsellers collection as the default landing point. Featured-product spotlights and "Shop the Look" modules cross-sell between price bands, guiding wallet buyers toward bags over time.

3. Building Trust for a New D2C Brand

Challenges

As a young direct-to-consumer label, RAQZ had no marketplace reputation to lean on. Shoppers hesitate to prepay thousands of rupees to an unfamiliar brand.

Solutions

We layered trust signals throughout the journey: verified customer reviews on product cards, transparent refund & exchange and shipping policies, secure checkout with recognizable digital wallets, and an active Instagram presence linked from the store. Free pan-India shipping removes the final objection at checkout.

4. Performance with Rich, High-Resolution Imagery

Challenges

Premium positioning demands large, detailed photography — but heavy images slow mobile load times, and most Indian D2C traffic arrives on mobile networks where every second costs conversions.

Solutions

We implemented responsive image delivery through Shopify's CDN — serving right-sized renditions per device — with lazy loading below the fold and compressed hero assets. The store keeps its editorial richness while staying fast on 4G connections.
